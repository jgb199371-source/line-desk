My bets

CLV % is your expected return at the closing market's fair (no-vig) price on the same line: positive means you got a better-than-fair price. Betting at exactly the book's closing price shows a small negative, because the book's cut is baked into it. If the line moved (e.g. you took +7 and it closed +5.5), CLV shows in points instead. Bets live in this browser on this computer, so export a backup now and then.
